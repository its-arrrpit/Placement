# Design Guidelines: Anti-"Vibecoding" Rules

To ensure the user interface looks bespoke, grounded, professional, and free of generic AI-generated landing page tropes, avoid the following 30 design clichés and patterns:

1. **No Harsh Gradients**: Avoid aggressive, high-contrast linear/radial gradient fills on buttons, backgrounds, or text.
2. **Avoid Generic/Overused Icons (e.g. Lucide Defaults)**: Use purposeful, custom, or cohesive icon styles; avoid throwing random Lucide icons as card decoration.
3. **No Pure Blinding White Backgrounds**: Use intentional, layered surface tones, warm or cool neutrals (e.g., `#fafafa`, `#f8f9fa`, slate/zinc tints) rather than raw `#ffffff` glare.
4. **No Rainbow Coloring**: Maintain a disciplined, restricted palette (1–2 brand tones max) rather than multi-colored badges and pastel rainbows.
5. **No Clunky Drop Shadows**: Avoid heavy, generic, dark blur shadows (`0 10px 25px rgba(...)`). Use subtle, multi-layered ambient elevations or crisp 1px borders.
6. **No Formulaic 3-Feature Cards in a Row**: Design layouts tailored to actual data and workflow rather than the mandatory triple-card marketing row.
7. **No Emojis as UI Icons or Section Headers**: Use professional typography and iconography, not inline emojis (🚀, 💡, 🔥, etc.).
8. **No Overdone Liquid Glass / Glassmorphism**: Avoid excessive backdrop-blur, frosted glass panels, and milky semi-transparent borders.
9. **No Em Dashes (—) in Marketing Copy**: Avoid clichéd AI writing cadence and punctuation tropes.
10. **Avoid Default AI Font Triad (Inter / Geist / Space Grotesk)**: Choose characterful, project-appropriate typography or clean system-native font stacks.
11. **No Colored Left Accent Stripes**: Avoid the standard colored vertical border strip on card edges.
12. **No Fake Testimonials**: Do not generate synthetic customer reviews or avatar quote blocks.
13. **No Gimmicky Bento Grids**: Structure data intuitively according to hierarchy and utility, not arbitrarily shaped grid boxes.
14. **No Decorative Fake Terminal Windows**: Avoid faux command prompt/code terminal widgets (`npm install ...`).
15. **No "It's not X, it's Y" Copywriting**: Avoid repetitive AI sales pitches and rhetorical tropes.
16. **No Generic Checkmark Bullet Lists**: Use structured metadata, comparison tables, or clear typographic hierarchy.
17. **No Clichéd 3 Pricing Tiers**: Avoid the cookie-cutter tier grid with a highlighted "Most Popular" card unless genuinely requested.
18. **Include Real Product Context / Demos**: Focus on functional, interactive data tables, workflows, and actual content rather than generic placeholders.
19. **No Excessive Soft/Pill Corner Radii**: Use clean, refined, structural radii (e.g., 4px–8px) rather than hyper-rounded bubble corners everywhere.
20. **Avoid the Clichéd Purple + Black Theme**: Steer clear of the saturated purple/violet-on-black AI dark mode default.
21. **Include Thoughtful Loading States & Skeleton Loaders**: Never leave blank voids, abrupt pop-ins, or generic spinners where skeleton loaders are appropriate.
22. **No Floating Radial Glow Orbs / Blurred Gradient Blobs**: Keep backgrounds grounded, crisp, and purposeful.
23. **No Generic Background Dot Grids**: Avoid subtle SVG dot grids or canvas grid lines used purely as filler.
24. **No Sparkle / Magic Icons (✨)**: Avoid decorative AI sparkle stars and magic wand glyphs.
25. **No Animated Bouncing/Sliding Arrows**: Avoid attention-seeking micro-arrows on links and CTAs.
26. **Include Real / Credible Footer Links (TOS, Privacy)**: Ensure legal/policy infrastructure is respected rather than ignored.
27. **Privacy Policy**: Provide realistic data and privacy context where required.
28. **No Flamboyant Card Hover Animations**: Avoid cards that scale up, pop out, or glow on hover; keep interactions subtle, snappy, and functional.
29. **No Screaming Neon Colors**: Avoid oversaturated electric greens, cyans, and hot pinks.
30. **No Washed-Out Basic Pastel Badges**: Avoid generic pale pink/blue/green tag pills with high saturation text.
